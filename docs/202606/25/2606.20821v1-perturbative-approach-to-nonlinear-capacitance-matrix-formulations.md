## 摘要
我们研究了一个在三维高对比度夹杂物上具有立方非线性的非线性亥姆霍兹系统，以及当对比度 $δ$ 趋于零时出现的孤子。利用狄利克雷到诺伊曼算子和电容形式，我们发展了一个微扰级联，将共振频率和场以 $\sqrtδ$ 的幂次展开。我们的主要结果是与一个有限离散非线性电容系统之间严格的双向对应：每个离散解提升为一个连续孤子（一个在 $\sqrtδ$ 中解析的收敛展开），并且每个具有自然亚波长标度的连续族都约化为一个离散族。该构造是算法化的，在亚波长和非亚波长区间都给出高阶修正，后者通过频率依赖的电容矩阵实现。我们用数值方法展示了该理论，并刻画了对称二聚体中的对称性破缺分岔。

## Abstract
We study a nonlinear Helmholtz system with cubic nonlinearity on high-contrast inclusions in three dimensions, and the solitons that emerge as the contrast $δ$ tends to zero. Using the Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator and a capacitance formalism, we develop a perturbative cascade that expands the resonant frequency and field in powers of $\sqrtδ$. Our main result is a rigorous two-way correspondence with a finite discrete nonlinear capacitance system: every discrete solution lifts to a continuous soliton (a convergent expansion, analytic in $\sqrtδ$), and every continuous family with the natural subwavelength scaling reduces to a discrete one. The construction is algorithmic, giving higher-order corrections in both the subwavelength and non-subwavelength regimes, the latter via a frequency-dependent capacitance matrix. We illustrate the theory numerically and characterise a symmetry-breaking bifurcation in a symmetric dimer.
