## 摘要
我们研究由平衡正折射率材料（PIM）和负折射率材料（NIM）构成的非厄米反对称宇称-时间（APT）光子学中的波反射。其中一类满足幺正关系，因此波阻抗匹配（WIM）不仅与偏振无关，而且可以实现全向。值得注意的是，该结果与系统尺寸、材料参数和偏振均无关。当涉及增益或损耗时，任何非厄米系统都无法支持WIM；相反，在某些系统构型、特定入射角和偏振依赖条件下，会出现反射谷，即最小反射率。我们的发现与宇称-时间（PT）对称范式形成对比，后者中著名的例外点可视为WIM。此外，在反射谷附近，反射相位发生跳变，为在APT系统中观察显著的Goos-Hänchen（GH）光束位移提供了机会。我们提供了相关系统并对GH位移进行了数值验证。同时，我们推导了反射谷关于入射角、工作波长和材料参数的闭式表达式。为了模拟APT光子学的角色散电磁响应，我们讨论了一种由空间对称多层板构成的介电异质结构，以在特定角度范围内对各APT组件实现相同的反射和透射。

## Abstract
Wave reflection by non-Hermitian antisymmetric parity-time (APT) photonics made of a balanced positive index material (PIM) and negative index material (NIM) is investigated. As one class of them satisfies a unitary relation, wave impedance matching (WIM) can be achieved not only for polarization independence but also for omnidirection. Remarkably, the result is independent of system sizes, material parameters, and polarization. As gain or loss is involved, any non-Hermitian systems are unable to support WIM; instead, there occurs a reflection dip, i.e., a minimum reflectance, at some system configurations, a certain incident angle, and polarization dependence.   Our finding is in contrast to the parity time (PT) symmetric paradigms, in which the well-known exceptional point can be recognized as a WIM. Additionally, in the vicinity of a reflection dip, it is accompanied by a jump of reflection phase, offering an opportunity to observe a significant Goos-Hanchen (GH) beam shift in APT systems. We provide associated systems and numerically verify the GH shifts.   Meanwhile, we derive a closed-form expression for a reflection dip in terms of incident angle, operating wavelengths, and material parameters. To imitate the electromagnetic responses of APT photonics for their angular dispersion, we discuss a dielectric heterostructure consisting of spatially symmetric multilayer slabs so as to have the same reflection and transmission for each APT component over a specific angular spread.
